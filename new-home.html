Financial Express
Leadership Awards

The Financial Express Leadership Awards honour India’s most dynamic business leaders, transformative organisations and groundbreaking ideas shaping the future of business.

At a time when industries are being disrupted, markets are being redefined and leadership demands are higher than ever, the FE Leadership Awards aim to recognise those who are not just navigating change — but driving it. These awards celebrate individuals and institutions whose bold decisions, strategic clarity and unwavering commitment to excellence are setting new benchmarks for Indian enterprise.

In an era marked by intense competition and rapid technological shifts, businesses need leaders who can inspire teams, build resilient organisations and create meaningful impact on society and the economy. The FE Leadership Awards honour precisely this calibre of leadership — the ones rewriting rules, redefining possibilities and delivering performance with purpose.

Associate Partners Aspect Global

Bridging Traditions, Building Tomorrow Aspect Global is a Mumbai-headquartered diversified business group with an entrepreneurial...

Aspect Global is a Mumbai-headquartered diversified business group with an entrepreneurial legacy dating back to 1956. From its roots as a family enterprise, the group has evolved into a business organisation with an international presence, guided by integrity, innovation and a commitment to long-term value creation.

Under the leadership of Aksha Kamboj, Group Chairperson & Managing Director, and Mohit Kamboj Bharatiya, Group Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director – Aspect Realty, Aspect continues to expand its portfolio through strategic investments, professional management and disciplined execution.

The group’s interests span bullion and refinery, real estate, infrastructure, industries, hospitality, quick-service restaurants, energy, sports and entertainment. Its businesses bring together consumer brands, development expertise and industrial capabilities, while Aspect Foundation supports community initiatives across education, healthcare, sustainability and social development.

With operations spanning India and international markets, Aspect combines the strengths of its individual businesses with a shared focus on quality, customer trust and responsible growth. Its approach centres on identifying opportunities, building capable teams and strengthening execution across the portfolio.

Guided by its philosophy, “Bridging Traditions, Building Tomorrow,” Aspect Global builds on its legacy while investing in businesses and opportunities that support sustained growth and meaningful impact.
