AWARDS

The FE India Leadership Awards honour India’s most dynamic business leaders, transformative organisations and groundbreaking ideas shaping the future of business.

At a time when industries are being disrupted, markets are being redefined and leadership demands are higher than ever, the FE India Leadership Awards aim to recognise those who are not just navigating change — but driving it. These awards celebrate individuals and institutions whose bold decisions, strategic clarity and unwavering commitment to excellence are setting new benchmarks for Indian enterprise.

In an era marked by intense competition and rapid technological shifts, businesses need leaders who can inspire teams, build resilient organisations and create meaningful impact on society and the economy. The FE India Leadership Awards honour precisely this calibre of leadership — the ones rewriting rules, redefining possibilities and delivering performance with purpose.

Sponsor TVS Motor Company

World's third largest two- and three-wheeler manufacturer, headquartered in Chennai.

TVS Motor Company (BSE: 532343, NSE: TVSMOTOR) is the world's third largest automaker in the two- and three-wheeler segment, focused on mobility solutions that are exciting, responsible, sustainable and safe. Headquartered in Chennai, the company is known for high-quality, high-value-for-money products, with more than 63 million customers riding a TVS vehicle worldwide.

TVS Motor's strength lies in its extensive research and development, and it operates a nationwide network of over 3,500 certified touch-points across four manufacturing facilities in India and Indonesia. Group companies include Norton Motorcycles in the UK and TVS e-bike Company AG in Switzerland. It was the first two-wheeler manufacturer to win the Deming Prize for quality excellence, and has ranked No. 1 in the J.D. Power Customer Service Satisfaction Survey.
