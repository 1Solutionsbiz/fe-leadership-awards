Financial Express Leadership Awards 2026

Criteria

What does it take to be nominated?

The Financial Express Leadership Awards recognise both individual leaders and the organisations they run, across eleven categories spanning leadership, finance, innovation and impact. Eligibility is intentionally broad — what matters most is the strength of the story behind the nomination, not the size of the company behind it.

Full entry guidelines and the nomination form will be published closer to the event. The notes below are a starting guide to who each category is designed for.

Eligibility, by category

Nominations are open to companies and individual business leaders operating in India, across every sector — from listed conglomerates to founder-led start-ups. Both self-nominations and third-party nominations, by industry bodies, colleagues or media, are accepted.

Lifetime Achievement, CEO of the Year, CFO of the Year and Young Turks recognise individual leaders. Young Turks is open specifically to leaders under 40; the other leadership categories carry no age restriction.

MNC of the Year, MSME Company of the Year, Start-up of the Year, Emerging Entrepreneur of the Year, Innovation of the Year and Sustainability Leader / Company evaluate the organisation itself. MSME Company of the Year follows India's official MSME classification; Start-up of the Year and Emerging Entrepreneur of the Year are open to companies in the early stages of their journey.

A completed nomination form together with supporting evidence — financials, citations, references — as applicable to the category. Submissions are reviewed and shortlisted by knowledge partner EY ahead of the jury round.

Shortlisted by Knowledge Partner · EY

Nominations for the inaugural Financial Express Leadership Awards open closer to the event on 15th September 2026 in Mumbai. This page will be updated with the detailed eligibility checklist and nomination form as they're finalised.
