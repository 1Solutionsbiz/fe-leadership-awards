About Us

The Financial Express Leadership Awards is India's newest platform for honouring the leaders redefining what business success looks like. Presented by Financial Express — India's trusted voice in business journalism for over six decades — the Awards bring together CEOs, founders and institutions across ten categories, judged by a panel of India's most respected business minds.

With a reach that spans print, digital, social and live events, Financial Express engages business leaders, policymakers, entrepreneurs and investors across every sector — a platform built on a reputation for factual, insightful reporting.

Financial Express is more than a newspaper; it's a trusted voice for India's business community. The Leadership Awards extend that legacy, recognising performance with purpose — leaders inspiring teams and building resilience, creating value beyond the balance sheet, and rewriting the rules of what's possible.

Celebrating Vision. Honoring Impact. Powering India's Next Chapter.

On 15th September 2026 in Mumbai, we'll bring together CEOs, policymakers, founders and industry leaders for an evening built around ten categories — from Lifetime Achievement to Innovation of the Year — each recognising a different facet of modern leadership.

Thought Leadership Platform
Recognising visionaries driving innovation, growth and transformation.
Multi-Platform Visibility
Exposure through FE's print, digital, social and event ecosystem.

Every nominee is judged against a rigorous, common bar: leadership & governance benchmarking, innovation & sustainability metrics, business performance analysis — shortlisted by knowledge partner EY — and real-world impact assessment. The final panel is chaired by Kumar Mangalam Birla, Chairman of the Aditya Birla Group.

Financial Express Leadership Awards: recognition built for the way leadership actually shows up now.
